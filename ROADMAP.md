# Research Roadmap

This roadmap presents the planned stages of the doctoral research. Dates may be refined according to data availability, institutional review, analytical findings, and thesis committee recommendations.

## Timeline: November 2025–December 2026

| Period | Stage | Main deliverables | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nov–Dec 2025 | Research design refinement | Final research questions, conceptual model, source inventory, initial protocol | Completed / under review |
| Jan–Mar 2026 | Data acquisition and governance | Mortality, population, sports infrastructure, GeoSampa, and vulnerability datasets; backup structure | In progress |
| Apr–Jun 2026 | Data engineering | Extraction, cleaning, standardization, geocoding, consolidation, and quality reports | In progress |
| Jul–Aug 2026 | Descriptive and exploratory analysis | Temporal trends, spatial distribution, missingness analysis, descriptive tables and maps | Planned |
| Sep–Oct 2026 | Statistical and spatial modeling | Correlation, regression, spatial autocorrelation, spatial regression, sensitivity analyses | Planned |
| Nov 2026 | Interpretation and validation | Robustness checks, triangulation, policy interpretation, reproducibility audit | Planned |
| Dec 2026 | Thesis consolidation | Final chapters, repository release, article manuscripts, defense preparation | Planned |

## Analytical milestones

### Milestone 1 — Reproducible data foundation

- Complete provenance records for all datasets
- Preserve raw files without manual alteration
- Produce standardized annual and territorial datasets
- Publish the data dictionary and quality-control rules

### Milestone 2 — Temporal analysis

- Calculate annual premature NCD mortality indicators
- Calculate infrastructure supply indicators
- Compare pre- and post-2030 Agenda periods
- Perform sensitivity analyses excluding 2020–2021 when epidemiologically justified

### Milestone 3 — Spatial analysis

- Harmonize territorial identifiers and geographic boundaries
- Produce choropleth maps and exploratory spatial data analysis
- Estimate global and local spatial autocorrelation
- Evaluate spatial regression models where assumptions support their use

### Milestone 4 — Policy synthesis

- Relate findings to SDG target 3.4
- Discuss urban equity and territorial disparities
- Translate findings into evidence-based recommendations for health, sports, and urban planning policies
